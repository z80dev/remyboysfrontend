# THE HAIRTRIX — a Remy Boys cyber-thriller (film #36)

Genre gap filled: cyber-thriller / bullet-time action (THE HAIRTRIX ≠ remy-2077, which is a
15s gameplay capture). 2D filmkit cinematic in the lineage of FADE TO BALD / HAIRPERA /
EXTRA-BALDESTRAL. Target **90 s** (88–94 acceptable), 960×540 pixel stage → 2× nearest →
1080p60. Green-phosphor grade over dark scenes; restrained bloom.

**Lore:** hairy Remys = heroes (The Flow, the resistance). Bald Remys = the Cabald
(the Agents: identical suits, shades, earpiece). Original names/lines only — parody twists
of iconic beats, never copied quotes, logos or melodies.

## Story spine (screenplay may compress but NOT drop the starred set pieces)
1. *Cold open* — black + phosphor terminal: "WAKE UP, FLOW…" / "FOLLOW THE WHITE HARE",
   digital rain of hair-strand glyphs. Title: THE HAIRTRIX.
2. *Contact* — TRIMNITY whisper / MOUSSE-PHEUS first contact.
3. *The Construct* ★ — white void, red pill (growth serum) vs blue pill (accept the shine).
   Mirror goes liquid; wake in the real world: bald farm pods. "Welcome to the real world."
4. *Training* — dojo spar vs MOUSSE-PHEUS; "there is no follicle" comb bend; rooftop jump program.
5. *Bullet time* ★ — rooftop: agent bullet wave, FLOW bends back; **frozen mid-air camera
   orbit** around suspended bullets with hair-part trails. The signature shot — spend ~8–10 s here.
6. *The lobby* ★ — pillar shootout, cartwheel behind cover, shells and stone dust raining.
7. *Subway duel* ★ — "you hear that, Mr. Flow? … a thousand clippers." Agent clipped by train,
   re-skinned as the next bald passenger.
8. *Payoff* — FLOW raises a hand, bullets freeze and drop; flies off, hair streaming.
   End card: FREE YOUR HAIR.

## Rules (all crew)
- Every frame pure function of t. No Math.random/Date.now; seed via rng/hash.
- Hit-stops via the proven STOPS/warp choreo (see skill + hairpera timeline). Score imports
  the same mapper — never hand-delay cues.
- Captions: ≥3 s full hold or reasoned per-caption minHold; reading text on overlay,
  16px+ at 960×540. Pixel HUD may live native.
- Feet anchors from the remy-bros rig (`loadRemy(id, near)`); letterbox safe band y ≤ ~470.
- Grade: green-tinted darkness in the Matrix world; harsh white in the Construct; cold blue
  in the real world. Keep FLOW's hair color readable through the grade.
- Score: dark propulsive electronic + industrial percussion + brooding pads; original motifs
  only; intentional ending. Derive every cue from timeline clocks.
